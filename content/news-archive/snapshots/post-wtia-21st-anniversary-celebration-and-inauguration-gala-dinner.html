WTIA “20+1st” Anniversary celebration and Inauguration Gala Dinner

This year marks the 20+1st year of establishment of WTIA.Throughout these years, WTIA keeps on taking the role of a super-connector in the Wireless Communication Industry, to bridge up the gap between government, enterprises, service providers and associations. Bring on the cheer with our 20+1st Anniversary celebration and Inauguration, we are going to organize a Gala Dinner on 29 September 2022.

Look forward to seeing you at the party.
*Seats Limited, will be on first come first serve basis

Date:             Thursday, 29 September 2022
Time:              7:00pm – 10:00 pm (Registration at 6:45pm)
Venue:           The Mira Hong Kong (118-130 Nathan Rd, Tsim Sha Tsui)
Ticket:             $1,750 per seat / $7,000 for half table(4 seats)

*20% off discount for WTIA Platinum Member
*10% off discount for WTIA Corporate and Startup Member

Registration: https://lnkd.in/g4_HjUry

WTIA 香港無線科技商會〈20 + 1 週年慶祝晚宴〉暨新一屆執行委員會就職典禮

為慶賀香港無線科技商會（WTIA） 成立 20 週年， WTIA 將舉辦 20 + 1 週年慶祝晚宴暨新一屆執行委員會就職典禮，在此歡迎各位與我們聚首一堂，歡慶及見證本會跨越二十載的里程碑。

誠邀各位出席，期待慶典當晚見到大家。
*名額有限，報名先到先得

日期：9 月 29 日（星期四）
時間： 晚上 7 時至 10 時
地點：美麗華酒店（尖沙咀彌敦道118-130號）

費用： HKD$ 1,750/ 位 或 HKD$ 7,000/半席（4 位）
*白金會員（Platinum）可享 8 折優惠
*企業會員(Corporate)及初創會員(Startup) 可享 9 折優惠
