

# WTIA Website — Modern Innovation Hub

A cinematic, tech-forward website for the Hong Kong Wireless Technology Industry Association, built around the provided copy and messaging pillars.

## Pages & Structure

### 1. Homepage
- **Cinematic hero** with animated gradient/particle background, bold headline ("Where Hong Kong's tech decision-makers connect"), subheadline, and dual CTAs: "Join WTIA" + "Talk to Us"
- **Social proof strip** — logos/partner badges with "Trusted by corporates, startups, and ecosystem partners across Hong Kong and Asia"
- **"Why WTIA" section** — 3 animated cards: Executive Network, Industry Platforms, Knowledge + Connection
- **3 Messaging Pillars** — Influence, Visibility, Access as bold section blocks with icons
- **Featured initiatives carousel** — ASA Awards, Tech to Connect, GenAI Courses
- **CTA banner** — "Ready to accelerate?" with membership + contact buttons

### 2. Membership Page
- Hero with headline: "Corporate Membership that converts technology into business growth"
- **Benefits grid** — 6 cards (Brand authority, Partnerships pipeline, Market intelligence, Employer branding, Speaking + visibility, Ecosystem credibility)
- **3 Membership tiers** — Enterprise (recommended badge), Corporate, Startup/Innovator — presented as pricing-style cards
- **"Who should join?"** section with audience segments
- **Application form** with smart microcopy, multi-select interests, and optional priority note

### 3. Projects & Awards Page
- Section intro with bold statement
- **ASA Awards feature block** — image/visual, description, CTA
- **Tech to Connect feature block** — image/visual, description, CTA
- Expandable for future initiatives

### 4. About / Executive Committee Page
- Mission statement with positioning copy
- Executive Committee member grid with photos, names, titles
- Organization history timeline (optional)

### 5. Contact / Talk to Us Page
- Partnership/sponsorship inquiry form
- Contact details and social links

## Global Elements
- **Navigation** — sticky header with logo, nav links (Home, Membership, Projects & Awards, About, Contact), "Join WTIA" CTA button
- **Footer** — one-line positioning tagline, quick links, social icons
- **Design system** — dark/premium theme with accent gradients (tech-forward blues, purples), smooth scroll animations, glassmorphism cards

## Design Direction
- Dark background with glowing accent colors (AI/tech aesthetic)
- Large typography, generous whitespace
- Subtle scroll-triggered animations on cards and sections
- Mobile-responsive throughout

All content uses the provided copy verbatim. No backend needed — purely a marketing/informational site with local form handling (toast confirmations).
