Mr. Nelson Tse is the Founder & CEO of Million Tech Development Limited. Drawing on

the company since its establishment in 1987 to become a premier provider of smart
technology services and automated solutions.

Under his strategic vision, Million Tech focuses on empowering organizations and
elevating operational efficiency through practical, high-impact innovation. The company
delivers end-to-end expertise across six core domains: Smart Health, Smart Education,
IoT, Vending & Kiosk Systems, Enterprise Solutions, and Robotics Labelling
Solutions. Driven by a commitment to quality and technology integration, Nelson
continues to lead Million Tech in shaping intelligent automation and digital
transformation for commercial, institutional, and public sector clients throughout Hong
Kong.
